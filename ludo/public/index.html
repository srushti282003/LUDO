How to Play Ludo

Goal

Move all 4 of your pieces from your yard, once around the board, and into your home column before your opponents.

Rolling

  • Roll a single die on your turn.
  • You need a 6 to bring a piece out of your yard.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you an extra roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.

Moving

  • Pieces move clockwise around the 52-square track, then up your own colored home column.
  • You must land exactly on the final home square — overshooting isn't allowed.

Capturing

  • Land exactly on a lone opponent piece to send it back to its yard. Capturing earns you an extra roll.
  • ★-marked squares and every color's own starting square are safe — pieces there can't be captured.

Blocks

  • Two or more of your pieces sharing a square form a block. Opponents cannot land on or capture a block.

Winning

The first player to bring all 4 pieces home wins. Play can continue to decide 2nd, 3rd and 4th place.

📖 Ludo Turn & Dice Rules

  • If a player does not roll the dice within 20 seconds, the system automatically rolls the dice.
  • After the dice result appears, if the player does not move a pawn within 20 seconds, the system automatically selects and moves a valid pawn.
  • If only one pawn is movable, it moves automatically without waiting for user input.
  • If a player rolls 6, they receive one extra turn.
  • If a player rolls three consecutive 6s, the third 6 is cancelled, no pawn moves, and the turn immediately passes to the next player.
  • If no valid pawn can move with the rolled number, the turn is automatically skipped after a short delay.
  • A turn timer is shown for every player with a countdown on screen.
  • If a player disconnects during their turn, the system uses auto-play until they reconnect or they are marked as surrendered after the timeout.
  • Users are strictly prevented from rolling or moving pieces when it is not their turn.
  • TWO SAME COLOUR PIECES CREATE A SAFE BLOCK CAN NOT CAPTURED .
  • All timers, dice results, and automatic actions are synchronized in real time for every player.
  • For AI turns, the dice stops on the final generated number and displays it clearly for 1–2 seconds before any pawn movement starts.
  • If multiple pawns are movable, all valid pawns are highlighted and the player can select any of them.
  • The dice is fully responsive on all mobile devices and screen sizes.
